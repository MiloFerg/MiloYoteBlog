Brainstorming

9/27/2026

Trying to design a website that's truly "my own" is more overwhelming than I was expecting. The technical barrier I can handle, at least I think I can, but the real killer was the freedom. I realized I don't really know what a site that's mine really looks like. I've seen loads of great examples on the web, and pinterest has a lot too- but I feel like trying to re-create those isn't really what I want either. They all seem to fit into pre-existing aesthetics, commercialized to high hell and they feel more like a mold to conform to than a true expression of the self. I'm trying not to conflate this with "originality" either, I know nothing I can do here is truly original.

I think the more damning realization is I don't know who I am as well as I thought. I like that though. It's a fresh start. Maybe this is what I needed. It's been really fun so far, after all!

So, with that out of the way, I think now would be a good time for an honest to god brainstorming sesh. I wanted to share my thoughts here so anyone can look at the process and know that people don't just pop out of nowhere with perfectly curated websites. I'll just write freeform first, then I'll summarize it for easy reference later.

Functionally, I want to have a portfolio gallery for my illustrations, background art, and animation. I might consolidate these all into one page with 3 sub pages, but I'm not yet sure how. I want my blog to be fun, personal, and a bit interactive if I can manage the coding. I'm still unsure if I want to do a full size blog where every single post is it's own dedicated page, or more of a micro-blog. I am definitely more attracted to the full size blog idea, even if it's more of a hassle, but I feel like that's the kind of stuff I've been craving since micro-blogging is already such a thing on the internet.

The visual design of my site has been tricky. I thought it would be the part I would clearly invision the best- but I just don't know what I would make in a vacuum. I think I want to use skies and textures, contrasted with fun and playful digital elements. I want a digital home feels Earth, and most importantly- the polar opposite of a v*be coded website.

In short:

  1. I want my portfolio to act as a curated gallery
  2. I want a playful, interactive, and anti-micro blog
  3. The design should embrace nature

That's all for now, time to actually work on this thing haha!!

Welcome to my blog!

9/6/2026

I wanted something that was completely my own, not overseen by any company, fully customizable and fun. (And because if I have to hear one more influencer tell me to "Follow my Substack" I'm gonna go insane)

Anyways, hope you enjoy your stay! I'm gonna try and make it a habit to update this blog weekly, at the very least. I'd love to include music I've been listening to, or a doodle, or something else fun. I'm not really sure what this kind of thing will be, but I think it'll be fun!

Ok, that's all, if you're reading this I hope you have a lovely day
